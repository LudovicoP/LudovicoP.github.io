Coursework 1 for MATH70076 Data Science, MSc in Statistics at Imperial College London. The work derives the quantile function for the Generalised Pareto Distribution via the probability integral transform, implements a custom GPD quantile function in R, and applies QQ-plot analysis across six datasets to identify parameter inconsistencies. A case study on river flow data investigates temporal stationarity, autocorrelation structure, mean residual life plots, and GPD tail fitting for extreme value modelling.

## Methods

- Generalised Pareto Distribution (GPD) theory and quantile functions
- Probability integral transform for sampling
- QQ-plot diagnostics for distribution fitting
- Extreme value analysis: mean residual life, threshold selection
- Temporal stationarity and autocorrelation analysis
