MSc Statistics summer research project at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Luati. The project investigates robust filtering of a time-varying location signal from heavy-tailed (Student-t) observations using particle methods, score-driven recursions, and near-exact grid-based benchmarks.

Three core research aims: (i) near-exact 1D benchmarking of particle filter reconstruction accuracy under Density2 limit-Gaussian innovations, (ii) operationalising the threshold where Kalman and score-driven approximations become adequate substitutes, and (iii) characterising how identifiability of the tail parameter degrades as the distribution approaches Gaussian.

*PDF available upon request.*

## Methods

- Bootstrap and auxiliary particle filters with ESS diagnostics
- Particle Marginal Metropolis-Hastings (PMMH) for Bayesian parameter learning
- Score-driven (GAS) observation-driven filtering baseline
- 1D deterministic grid/quadrature filter as near-exact benchmark
- Profile likelihood and curvature analysis for tail parameter identifiability
