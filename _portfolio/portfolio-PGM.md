Two courseworks for Probabilistic Generative Models, MSc in Statistics at Imperial College London. CW1 covers Bernoulli mixture models and Gaussian process regression with full derivations and Python implementations. CW2 tackles deep generative models: variational autoencoders (VAEs) and generative adversarial networks (GANs) for image generation on MNIST and face datasets, with latent space analysis and generation quality evaluation.

## Methods

- Bernoulli mixture models with EM algorithm
- Gaussian process regression and kernel design
- Variational Autoencoders (VAE) with reparameterisation trick
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN)
- Latent space visualisation and interpolation
