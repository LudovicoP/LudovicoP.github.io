Coursework for Computational Statistics and Machine Learning, MSc in Statistics at Imperial College London. Part 1 implements and compares MCMC samplers for a multimodal target: random-walk Metropolis-Hastings (with and without support constraints via log-transform), and parallel tempering. Convergence diagnostics include trace plots, ACF, and confidence intervals for expectations. Part 2 develops quantile regression models for house price prediction using asymmetric loss, with extensions to quadratic/interaction terms, ridge penalisation with cross-validation, and non-crossing quantile approaches.

## Methods

- Random-walk Metropolis-Hastings with adaptive proposals
- Parallel tempering for multimodal exploration
- MCMC diagnostics: trace plots, ACF, ESS, confidence intervals
- Quantile regression with asymmetric check loss
- Ridge-penalised quantile regression with k-fold CV
- Non-crossing quantile constraints
