Coursework for MATH70078 Fundamentals of Statistical Inference, MSc in Statistics at Imperial College London. The work presents rigorous derivations in Bayesian decision theory: computing Bayes actions and risks with and without data, proving that posterior-based decisions cannot perform worse than prior-based ones (value of information), and deriving highest-density regions as optimal set estimators under Lebesgue-penalised loss.

PDF: <a href="/pdfs/StatInference-ICL.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">View PDF</a>

## Methods

- Bayesian decision theory: loss functions, Bayes risk, optimal actions
- Prior vs. posterior risk comparison and value of information
- Highest-density region derivation under set estimation loss
- Formal measure-theoretic proofs
