Two assignments for MATH70013 Advanced Simulation Methods, MSc in Statistics at Imperial College London. Assignment 1 implements Metropolis-Hastings for parameter inference in a linear Gaussian state-space model, using the Kalman filter to compute exact likelihoods. Posterior marginals, credible intervals, trace plots, and ACF diagnostics are reported with effective sample sizes. Assignment 2 extends to particle filtering, sequential Monte Carlo, and advanced simulation techniques.

## Methods

- Random-walk Metropolis-Hastings with Kalman filter likelihood
- Posterior diagnostics: trace plots, ACF, ESS, credible intervals
- Linear Gaussian state-space models
- Particle filtering and sequential Monte Carlo
